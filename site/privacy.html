Anthem Gym Timer Privacy Support

Privacy

Anthem Gym Timer does not have accounts, and it does not send your gym’s timer setup anywhere.

What stays on the device

The gym name, colors, logo, and saved timer presets are stored only on that phone or TV. On a phone, the logo is read from your photo library or files, resized on the device, and kept there. On Apple TV and Fire TV, a phone on the same Wi-Fi can send a logo to that TV. The TV serves the page itself. The photo is not sent to Anthem Gym Timer or to any other service.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, or email.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK.
  • No network request that sends timer, branding, or logo data to Anthem Gym Timer or any other service. A phone and a TV on the same Wi-Fi can pass a logo between them. That exchange stays on the local network.
  • No remote push registration. Alerts are local notifications scheduled on the device so the clock can chime if the phone locks.

Photos

On iPhone, the photo library permission is only so you can pick a gym logo. The app does not browse the library on its own and does not use the camera or microphone. Apple TV and Fire TV have no photo library in this app. A phone browser sends the logo to the TV over Wi-Fi, and the TV stores it.

Outside this app

Apple and Amazon can collect their own diagnostics for the stores and the operating system. That is their software, not Anthem Gym Timer. This policy covers the Anthem Gym Timer app itself.
