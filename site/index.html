A gym clock for intervals, rounds, and countdowns. Any gym can put its own name and colors on the screen.

On the clock

  • For Time, AMRAP, EMOM, Tabata, and custom intervals.
  • BJJ, boxing, MMA, and Muay Thai rounds, plus rounds you save yourself.
  • A countdown with a lead-in and warning marks.
  • The same timer on iPhone, Apple TV, and Fire TV.

There is no account. The gym name, colors, and logo stay on the device. A phone can send a logo to a TV on the same Wi-Fi, and that photo does not go to a server. See the privacy policy.
