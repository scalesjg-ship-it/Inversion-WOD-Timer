### Description

Americana is a gym clock for intervals, rounds, and countdowns. It is built for any gym. The default look is dark and high contrast. Put your own gym name, colors, and logo on the screen. That branding stays on the device. On Apple TV and Fire TV, scan the code on Brand with a phone on the same Wi-Fi to send a logo. That transfer stays on the local network.

CrossFit-style modes: For Time with an optional cap, AMRAP, EMOM, Tabata, and intervals you set yourself. Every mode can start with a lead-in, including zero.

Martial-arts rounds: BJJ, boxing, MMA, and Muay Thai, plus round lengths you save. A separate countdown takes hours, minutes, and seconds, with quick adds and warning marks at one minute, thirty seconds, ten seconds, and the last three seconds.

The digits stay large in portrait and landscape. Work, rest, lead-in, and warning each have their own color. Tap to pause. The phone stays awake while the clock runs, and local notifications can chime if the screen locks.

The same timer runs on iPhone, Apple TV, and Fire TV. On a TV, the remote starts and pauses, skips time, and resets. There is no account and no tracking.
